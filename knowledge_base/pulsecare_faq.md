# PulseCare Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I book an appointment with a doctor?
To book an appointment:
1. Log in to your PulseCare patient account.
2. Navigate to the Doctors page or use the AI Assistant to locate a specialist based on your needs.
3. Select an available date and time slot within the doctor's working schedule.
4. Provide a brief reason for your visit and confirm your booking. You will see your booking instantly under "My Appointments".

## What are the clinic hours and locations?
- PulseCare Main Hospital Facility is open Monday through Friday from 08:00 AM to 06:00 PM, and Saturday from 08:30 AM to 02:00 PM.
- Walk-in Urgent Care is open 7 days a week from 07:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Emergency Department (ER) operates 24/7/365.

## What is the appointment cancellation policy?
- Patients may cancel or reschedule appointments without penalty up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled time via the Patient Portal.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance may incur a nominal $25 late cancellation fee.

## Does PulseCare accept insurance?
Yes. PulseCare is in-network with major insurance providers including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and Medicaid. Copays and deductibles depend on your individual plan. For uninsured or self-pay patients, standard transparent fee schedules apply.

## How does the PulseCare AI Triage work?
The AI Triage analyzes your reported symptoms, duration, and clinical risk factors. It categorizes your case into:
- **Emergency**: Immediate call to 911 or visit to the Emergency Room.
- **Urgent**: Prompt consultation within 12-24 hours at our Urgent Care clinic.
- **Non-Urgent**: Standard appointment scheduled with a specialist within 1-3 days.
- **Self-Care**: Mild symptoms managed with rest, hydration, and monitoring.
The triage tool is an assessment aid and does not replace emergency clinical evaluation.

## Can I consult a doctor online via Telehealth?
Yes! PulseCare offers Telehealth Virtual Consultations for non-emergency acute concerns, prescription refills, and follow-ups. Telehealth visits last 20 minutes with a base fee of $75 and can be scheduled through the portal.
