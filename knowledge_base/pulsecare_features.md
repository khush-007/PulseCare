# PulseCare System Features & Architecture Guide

## Overview of PulseCare
PulseCare is an enterprise-grade Smart Healthcare Appointment & Triage System. It connects patients with accredited medical specialists, facilitates seamless clinic appointment scheduling, provides instant AI-assisted clinical triage, and delivers patient-centric health intelligence.

## Patient Portal Features
- **Appointment Scheduling**: Patients can explore doctors across five primary specialties (Cardiology, Neurology, Pediatrics, Dermatology, Orthopedics) and General Medicine, view consultation fees and room assignments, and schedule visits.
- **My Appointments Dashboard**: Authenticated patients can view upcoming and past appointments, appointment statuses (`scheduled`, `completed`, `cancelled`), and cancellation options.
- **Medical Services Directory**: Transparent pricing and duration for clinical services including Comprehensive Cardiac Evaluations ($250), Full-Body Skin Screenings ($160), Neurological EEGs ($320), Well-Child Checkups ($120), Orthopedic Joint Assessments ($210), and Telehealth Virtual Consultations ($75).
- **AI Clinical Triage Integration**: Patients experiencing acute symptoms can describe what they feel and receive automated severity stratification: Emergency, Urgent, Non-Urgent, or Self-Care.

## Doctor Portal Features
- **Provider Profiles**: Doctors maintain qualifications, clinical background, consultation fees, active clinic room numbers, and available working hours (e.g., Dr. Sarah Smith, MD FACC Harvard, Suite 204).
- **Schedule Management**: Doctors can track upcoming patient appointments, patient symptom summaries, and clinical reason for visits.
- **Department Coordination**: Departmental triage linking patients directly to corresponding specialists based on clinical indicators.
