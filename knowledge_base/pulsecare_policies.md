# PulseCare Clinical & Privacy Policies

## Patient Privacy & HIPAA Compliance Policy
PulseCare is fully compliant with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) and international patient data security regulations.
- All personal identifiable information (PII) and protected health information (PHI) are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3.
- Only authorized clinical staff assigned to a patient's care team have access to medical charts.
- The AI chatbot never reveals one patient's health records or appointments to another user. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) is enforced at the database and application levels.
- Patients may request complete deletion of their chatbot conversation history at any time using the "Clear History" feature.

## Emergency Medical Protocol
PulseCare AI Chatbot is an informative conversational agent and MUST NEVER be used as a substitute for emergency services.
- If a user reports life-threatening symptoms—such as crushing chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), severe uncontrolled bleeding, anaphylaxis, or thoughts of self-harm—the system immediately short-circuits to emergency guidance.
- Emergency guidance provides local numbers (911 in North America, 112 in Europe, 102/108 in India, or 988 for suicide/crisis lifeline).

## Prescription & Medication Guidance Policy
- The AI chatbot does NOT prescribe medication, adjust dosages, or endorse off-label drug combinations.
- Information provided regarding medicines (e.g., Amoxicillin, Lisinopril, Metformin) is strictly educational, explaining standard indications, common side effects, and warnings.
- Patients requiring prescription renewals or dosage alterations must schedule a formal consultation with their PulseCare physician.
